Raw-data cache of the rdfc2im hybrid HumanMine demo, 114-gene panel (release demo-data-2026-11-18); notice written
2026-11-18, second pass, im-9n4.45
==============================================================================================================

Each directory is a cache group: a fetch of an upstream source, cut to the gene panel where the group says scope=panel
in its META.json (the data were changed by filtering or by re-writing RDF query results as tables; rows were left out,
none was edited). Nothing here is original to this project; each group stays under its upstream licence and must be
credited to it. The licence of each source was read from the source's own page or file on 2026-11-18; the record with
links and wording is rdfc2im/data/sources.yaml, stock_sources.yaml, other_licences.yaml and docs/LICENCES.md.

  https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/uniprot/current_release/knowledgebase/complete/README

  https://geneontology.org/docs/go-citation-policy/

  https://reactome.org/license

  HGNC gene nomenclature (RDF Portal), panel genes.  CC0 1.0: "all data is released under the Creative Commons Public
  Domain (CC0) License". Attribution is recommended: Seal RL et al., Genenames.org: the HGNC and PGNC resources in 2026,

ncbigene, gene-id-lookup, grch38-chromosome-lengths
  NCBI Gene (RDF Portal) and NCBI gene_info for the ID resolver, panel genes.  NCBI "places no restrictions on the use or
  distribution of the data contained therein"; no licence is named.  Source: National Library of Medicine, National Center
  for Biotechnology Information.  Some records may carry rights claimed by their submitters, which NCBI cannot waive.
  The chromosome lengths (grch38-chromosome-lengths) are the GRCh38 sequence lengths as served by the HumanMine JBrowse
  configuration; no licence is stated there.
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/

clinvar
  ClinVar (RDF Portal), panel genes.  NCBI's general policy places no restrictions on use or distribution (see above);
  ClinVar's own data-use page (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/docs/maintenance_use/) could not be read. Source:
  NCBI ClinVar, National Library of Medicine.

hpo
  Human Phenotype Ontology (hp), the whole ontology re-written as tables (terms, alternative ids, synonyms, SNOMED CT
  cross-references, superclasses); nothing left out or changed.  This product uses the Human Phenotype Ontology (version
  information as in the cache). Find out more at http://www.human-phenotype-ontology.org .  The HPO licence requires the
  Human Phenotype Ontology Consortium to be acknowledged and cited, the date or version of displayed files to be shown,
  and that "neither the content of the HPO file(s) nor the logical relationships embedded within the HPO file(s) be altered
  in any way".
  https://hpo.jax.org/license

bluegenes-tools, bluegenes-tools-patched
  The 18 BlueGenes tool npm packages (MIT x16, LGPL-3.0 x1, LGPL-2.1 x1); the patched set is the same packages with

  cytoscape-interaction-network-viewer (LGPL-3.0) and protvista (LGPL-2.1, no LICENSE file in the package); the source of

humanmine-model
  The HumanMine data model (humanmine_model.xml and .json), part of the InterMine/HumanMine software; it is not data
  from any source above and no separate licence was read for it in this pass.
