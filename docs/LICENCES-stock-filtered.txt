Filtered stock-source data for the rdfc2im hybrid HumanMine testbed (corrected 2026-11-18, second pass, im-9n4.45)
==================================================================================================================

Each directory stock-<source>/ is a subset of an upstream file, cut to the human genes (and the ids that tie to
them) the build needs by tools/subset_raw.py: the data were changed by filtering (rows left out; no row was edited).
Nothing here is original to this project; each part stays under its upstream licence and must be credited to it.
META.json in each group records a short licence key. The licence of each source was read from the source's own page
or file on 2026-11-18; the record, with the links and wording, is rdfc2im/data/stock_sources.yaml and docs/LICENCES.md.

This archive has no stock-panther group: PANTHER's terms could not be read, so its data are not redistributed here.

Not in this archive: PANTHER (stock-panther: terms not readable), OMIM, DisGeNET and KEGG (decision D-7 of the testbed

whether the licence was verified: docs/LICENCES.md and licence_status in rdfc2im/data/stock_sources.yaml do.
